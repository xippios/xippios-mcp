- A call over a source's own MCP ceiling is refused through the host, where 0.1.0 served it:
  an endpoint applies each package's option hooks with the defaults that the package's entry
  point parses from an empty command line, so a `usda-nass` split of a sixth year and a
  `usda-gats` series of a 25th month are refused, where 0.1.0 served the 20 years and 60
  months of their Python APIs. An endpoint of one package shows that package's instructions
  and version with an include or exclude list too, and a group's instructions give each
  package's own under a heading of its slug. A hook that refuses its defaults stops the host
  with exit status 2, naming the file, the endpoint and the package.
  (ADR-0001, https://github.com/xippios/xippios-mcp/issues/27,
  https://github.com/xippios/sdk-core/issues/485)
